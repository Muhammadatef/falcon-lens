# VISION

**Problem.** UAE residents get fake fine, delivery-fee, bank-OTP and UAE Pass links by SMS, WhatsApp and email. The pages look official and exist only for hours, and blocklists catch them late. Older and non-technical people get the worst of it: a warning in English and in jargon doesn't help them.

**Promise.** Paste anything suspicious and get a clear Arabic answer in seconds: what we found, why, and what to do now, including what to do if you already clicked.

**Users.** Residents (web PWA, share sheet, Chrome extension), and police or security operators (incident board, REST, MCP, STIX). Integration is ready to connect, not connected.

**Two agents.** The *Investigator* (Baseer | بصير) checks one input. The *Sentinel* (Dir' | درع) watches every check around the clock, links them into campaigns, blocks indicators internally and drafts takedowns for a human to approve.

**Voice.** Say "Hey Falcon" (يا صقر) and the assistant checks what you copied and narrates the live steps of both agents, in English or Arabic. Speech is recognised on our own machine; audio and transcripts are not stored or logged.

**Principles.** Proactive (catch zero-day pages through the brand-mismatch rule and campaigns), applicable (one command, runs on a laptop, sovereign mode), truthful (every number comes from an eval artifact), and defensive-only.
