## Findings
1. **(blocking, product meaning) `packages/falcon_core/src/falcon_core/i18n/ar.yaml:47` and `en.yaml:47` now disagree.**
   Arabic now says «الجهات الرسمية لا تطلبه منك برسالة أو اتصال» ("…don't ask you for it by message or call"), but English still says "Official bodies never ask for it." The verdict card is bilingual, so one reason key must mean the same thing in both languages. Your point is fair: you do type an OTP into your own bank's app. But the key point for the reader is that nobody official will ever *ask* you for it, and dropping «أبداً» makes that weaker. Here is a version that keeps both:
   - ar: `"الرسالة تطلب رمز التحقق. الجهات الرسمية لا تطلبه منك أبداً برسالة أو اتصال."` (72 chars, under the limit)
   - en (same PR, same key): `"The message asks for your verification code. Official bodies never ask for it in a message or call."`

   If you'd rather not touch `en.yaml`, revert line 47 to the old Arabic and say why in the PR. Either way is fine. They just need to match.
2. **(should fix) `domain.age.old` rationale (`ar.yaml:32`).** The PR table says «طويلة» "overstates a site that is 2–3 years old". However, this reason only appears when the domain is **older than 3650 days**: `scoring/arbiter.py:121` gives weight only for `age > 3650`, and `scoring/reasons.py:91` skips evidence with weight 0. So the old wording «منذ سنوات طويلة» was accurate and matched the English "for many years". Please either revert it, or use something clearer for an older reader such as «هذا الموقع مسجّل منذ أكثر من عشر سنوات.» (with the same change in `en.yaml`). Also update the PR table's reason so it doesn't mislead the next reader.
3. **(should fix) Stale notes in `data/brands.yaml`.** The Etisalat entry (`data/brands.yaml:201`) and the TAQA entry (`:161`) still say "name_ar not confirmed on page", even though you have now confirmed it. Please replace that phrase with the source, the way you did for Abu Dhabi Police. For example, Etisalat: `name_ar «اتصالات» as written on eand.ae/ar (2026-11-22)`, and TAQA: `name_ar «طاقة للتوزيع» as in the official app listing (Google Play com.ADDC.addcApp, 2026-11-22)`. Note: #22 rewrites the TAQA `unverified_notes` line too, and its text says "name_ar to be reviewed under C01". If #22 merges first, rebase and change that clause to the source above.
4. (nit) `DECISIONS.md`: the new line calls «لا يضمن» an "absolute claim". It isn't really one; it was a plain wording simplification. Please adjust the wording if you're editing the line anyway.
