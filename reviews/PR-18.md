## Findings

### Blocking
1. **Branch does not merge, no CI ran.** `merge-tree` shows conflicts in 19 files against `cb94a17`. The scoring/arbiter/i18n modules are now on main, from the arbiter merge. `make eval-gate` doesn't exist on this branch. *Fix:* rebase onto current main. Take main's scoring modules and re-apply only the cascade-specific changes, and take main's ratified CONTRACTS §Internal messages (merged with #4), adding "Events" and "Worker-side keys" as a delta. Then get `gh pr checks 18` green, including eval-gate. When #16 merges, `security/{http,ssrf}.py` and `services/egress/*` will also conflict: take #16's versions.

2. **MEDIUM: a transient sink error on the final turns into a second, degraded final, and that gets cached for 6 h.**
   - The cause: `engine.py:190-199` calls `cache.put` (→ `SET vc:<fp> <vid> EX 21600`) *before* `sink.emit`. If `sink.emit` then raises (`consumer.py:95-96`, SET/PUBLISH), the catch-all at `engine.py:231-235` re-scores with `internal_error:ConnectionError` and emits a *second* final under the same `verdict_id`. That overwrites `fl:verdict:last:<vid>` and the Postgres row with a degraded `suspicious`.
   - `RedisVerdictCache.get` (`consumer.py:62-72`) doesn't reject degraded docs, so every identical check for 6 h is re-issued as that degraded verdict.
   - Probe output: `published: [('T0',False,False), ('T1',False,False), ('T2',True,True,['internal_error:ConnectionError'])]`, then `second check: suspicious True ['internal_error:ConnectionError'] Verdict cache hit (from vd_…)` with `vc ttl: 21600`.
   - It also breaks "the worker fills `vc:` only for non-degraded finals" (CONTRACTS, Worker-side keys) and the docstring order at `consumer.py:3-9`, where `vc` is step 4, after PUBLISH.
   - *Fix:*
     - (a) run `cache.put` only after `sink.emit` returns, in the worker sink after PUBLISH, as documented;
     - (b) don't route sink or transport exceptions into the re-score path. Let them propagate to the worker, which can retry, see 3;
     - (c) `RedisVerdictCache.get` must return `None` for `degraded` docs;
     - (d) add a test with a sink whose final PUBLISH fails once.

3. **MEDIUM: infrastructure failures ack and drop the job, and there's no DLQ or reclaim for them.**
   - `consumer.py:223-224` acks in `finally`, so any exception from the cascade or sink acks the entry. With Redis PUBLISH failing (probe `sink_always_fails`): `read_batch raised ConnectionError`, `pending: 0`, `dead: 0`. The job is gone, no final is ever published, and the SSE client waits until its timeout. `fl:jobs:dead` only catches malformed jobs (`:200-204`).
   - Separately, `run_forever` re-reads only *its own* pending list (`:181`, id `0`). The consumer name is `worker-<hostname>` (`:129`), and that changes whenever a container is recreated. So entries left pending by a dead consumer are never reclaimed, because there's no `XAUTOCLAIM`.
   - *Fix:*
     - ack only after a final was published, or after the entry has been written to `fl:jobs:dead`;
     - on an exception, leave the entry pending;
     - add an `XAUTOCLAIM` sweep (min-idle about 60 s) and use the delivery count: after N deliveries (for example 3), XADD to `fl:jobs:dead` with the reason, publish a degraded final if possible, then ack;
     - add tests for "publish fails, so the entry stays pending" and "another consumer's idle entry is claimed and finished".
