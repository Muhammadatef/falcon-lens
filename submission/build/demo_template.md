2. **What about false positives on banks and government sites?** "On the frozen test split no official site was marked Dangerous; on the live full stack the false-alarm rate is above our target ({FP_SHORT}), mostly checks that could not finish, and that is our first fix."
3. **Do redirects, shorteners and cloaking fool it?** "The sandbox follows redirects and the final page is judged, and a cloaking check compares two loads of the page; we have not published a separate score for each, so we do not quote one."
4. **Do you cover search results, ads and Google Maps?** "Ready, needs a key: the connectors are written, no key is configured, so we show no result for them."
5. **Can it catch brand-new look-alike domains?** "Yes for pages people send us: {BRAND_SHORT}. {HUNTER_QA}"
6. **Does an LLM decide the verdict?** "No. The model reads and explains; a fixed arbiter sets the score; page text is quarantined; the red-team found no harm, and we state how many cases the model answered."
7. **What about data sovereignty?** "Every model call can stay on the machine: {SOVEREIGN_SHORT} Lookups about the checked site, such as RDAP and the sandbox's one passive visit, still use the internet through our egress proxy."
8. **Does it scale?** "By design: Redis streams and stateless workers, so you add workers. It is proven on one machine only, and we say so."
9. **What would a police pilot look like?** "The same stack inside your network, shadow mode first: your sign-on, your feeds, your security review. REST, MCP and STIX are ready to connect; nothing is connected today."
10. **What are the limits?** "Live false alarms above target, a fresh test of one run with unaudited labels, synthetic voice and red-team sets, and two things not measured yet: a native reader's rating of the Arabic and sandbox recall on labelled phishing pages."

## Before you go on stage

1. Read `submission/CHECKLIST.md`, section "Capabilities claimed". Cut any beat marked not shipped.
2. Clean state: `make demo-reset YES=1`. Start the planted campaign two minutes before the beat: `make replay RATE=2/s DURATION=2m`.
3. Tabs, in order: deck, `https://localhost:8443/` (home, voice), `/history`, `/campaigns`, `/results`, `/ops` (key typed before, not shown).
4. Copy the demo SMS (demo-kit item M1) to the clipboard. Run it once and confirm «خطِر».
5. Sound: test the alert sound and the falcon voice through the room speakers, and test the microphone at the stage distance.
6. Do Not Disturb on. Quit chat, mail and calendar. Never show `.env`, the operator key or the terminal history.
7. Rehearse twice with a timer. The close must start with twenty seconds left.
8. Come back from the browser to the deck when the demo window ends, with forty seconds left: the deck waits on the live-demo slide, so one click goes to the conclusion and one more to the close.
