## Brief Description (English)

Length: 592 of 600 characters, 4 sentences. Limit: two to four sentences.

```text
Falcon Lens is an Arabic-first AI agent for UAE residents: paste a suspicious link, message, email or image and get an evidence-backed verdict with what was found, why, and what to do now. A brand check flags lookalike pages even when no blocklist lists them; code, not a model, sets the score. A second agent, the Sentinel, links checks into campaigns and drafts takedowns for human approval. It can keep every model call local and offers REST and MCP interfaces that are ready to connect to police systems; it is an independent project, not connected to or affiliated with Abu Dhabi Police.
```
