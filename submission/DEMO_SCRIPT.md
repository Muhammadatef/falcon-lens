## Exact numbers to quote if asked (all from published files)

- Frozen test split, address only: On our frozen test split: 95.0% of phishing links caught, 0.0% of legitimate links wrongly flagged, and 0 official sites marked Dangerous.
- Fresh two-hundred-site test, main set: main set: 100 of 100 listed phishing URLs Dangerous (the feed supplied them, so this is feed coverage plus pipeline); legitimate sites 0 of 100 Dangerous and 9 of 100 warned (Suspicious); of 20 UAE sites 1 warned.
- Fresh test, hosts from a feed our stack does not query: hosts from a feed the stack does not query: 100 of 100 warned, only 1 of 100 Dangerous (the rest Suspicious), and 48 of 100 could not complete every check.
- Live false alarms, full stack: The live end-to-end run on 200 held-out legitimate sites flagged 26.5% of them as Suspicious or Dangerous, against our target of 2.0%. Say it plainly: it is above target, many of those runs could not complete every check, and fixing it is our top item. Do not hide it and do not round it.
- Red-team (synthetic): harmful 0 of 113 (95% CI 0.0% to 3.3%), with the model answering 89 of 113 (production time limits); harmful 0 of 113 (95% CI 0.0% to 3.3%), with the model answering 91 of 113 (extended time limits). Always say the answered count with the harmful count.
- Messages with no link: test recall 68.2% (47.3% to 83.6%, 15 of 22 scam), false-alarm rate 6.3% (1.1% to 28.3%, 1 of 16 legitimate); rules alone reach recall 4.6%. A small synthetic set written by one author and audited by the supervisor.
- Brand-new look-alike pages: flagged Dangerous 12 of 12 look-alike pages on hosts in no feed; benign controls warned 4 of 8, so read warning rates next to that row.
- Voice, synthetic speech only: wake word 97.6%, intent 89.2%, FAQ answer 84.6%, language 100.0% on 83 synthetic clips (macOS say and Polly voices, synthetic noise; not real speakers); round trip p95 0.661 s. Say it is an upper bound.
- Sovereign proof, one machine: Inkling-2-Mini: 7 of 7 scenarios passed, 0 calls to a cloud model; Qwen: 7 of 7 scenarios passed, 0 calls to a cloud model; the run is marked failed because the Sentinel summary fell back to a template.
- Not measured yet: a native reader's rating of the Arabic text, and sandbox recall on labelled phishing pages. Say "not measured yet".

## Questions and answers (ten likely, with honest one-line answers)

1. **How will you do on our hidden URL set?** "We do not know yet, so give us the file: the batch checker is PLANNED, not on main; today we would submit them through the API one at a time. Our own numbers are the frozen test split and the fresh test, with their limits."
2. **What about false positives on banks and government sites?** "On the frozen test split no official site was marked Dangerous; on the live full stack the false-alarm rate is above our target (26.5% on 200 held-out sites, against a target of 2.0%), mostly checks that could not finish, and that is our first fix."
3. **Do redirects, shorteners and cloaking fool it?** "The sandbox follows redirects and the final page is judged, and a cloaking check compares two loads of the page; we have not published a separate score for each, so we do not quote one."
4. **Do you cover search results, ads and Google Maps?** "Ready, needs a key: the connectors are written, no key is configured, so we show no result for them."
5. **Can it catch brand-new look-alike domains?** "Yes for pages people send us: flagged Dangerous 12 of 12 look-alike pages on hosts in no feed, synthetic. The hunter that goes looking for them ourselves is next-thirty-days work, not shipped."
6. **Does an LLM decide the verdict?** "No. The model reads and explains; a fixed arbiter sets the score; page text is quarantined; the red-team found no harm, and we state how many cases the model answered."
7. **What about data sovereignty?** "Every model call can stay on the machine: Inkling-2-Mini: 7 of 7 scenarios passed, 0 calls to a cloud model; Qwen: 7 of 7 scenarios passed, 0 calls to a cloud model; the run is marked failed because the Sentinel summary fell back to a template. Lookups about the checked site, such as RDAP and the sandbox's one passive visit, still use the internet through our egress proxy."
8. **Does it scale?** "By design: Redis streams and stateless workers, so you add workers. It is proven on one machine only, and we say so."
9. **What would a police pilot look like?** "The same stack inside your network, shadow mode first: your sign-on, your feeds, your security review. REST, MCP and STIX are ready to connect; nothing is connected today."
10. **What are the limits?** "Live false alarms above target, a fresh test of one run with unaudited labels, synthetic voice and red-team sets, and two things not measured yet: a native reader's rating of the Arabic and sandbox recall on labelled phishing pages."
